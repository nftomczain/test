Bundled here so GhostPoster can correctly render Polish diacritics
(ą ć ę ł ń ó ś ź ż) in generated PDFs regardless of which fonts are
installed on the end user's system — PyMuPDF's built-in base14 fonts
(Helvetica etc.) do not support these characters.
